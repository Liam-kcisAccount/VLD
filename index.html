What this site is

This is my Virtual Learning & Development portfolio for Kang Chiao International School, 2026–2027. It documents one thing: the year I spent teaching myself Python through freeCodeCamp, one supervised period a week, and how honestly I can say I ran that time myself.

Four pages beyond this one: a course log of what I actually did and when, a weekly reflection journal written right after each session, a short learner bio for next year's students, and the evidence — certificates, screenshots, and things I actually built.
